## Video 1: "From Illegal Casino To Real Business" (`uiIKhYWuO50`)

- **URL:** https://youtube.com/shorts/uiIKhYWuO50
- **Duration:** 68 seconds
- **Performance:** 1,096,452 views | 31,849 likes | 109 comments
- **Core Topic:** How the 3 stages of money laundering (**Placement**, **Layering**, **Integration**) work—and why cash-intensive fronts still get caught when their unit economics don't add up.

| **00:49.2 – 00:55.1** | **Stage 3: Integration** | *"Then, the money comes home looking innocent, and you finally buy the Lamborghini. This is called integration."* | Resolves the earlier Lamborghini setup and introduces Technical Term #3 (**Integration**). |
